PRODUCT OVERVIEW · NON-TECHNICAL

The HRMS SaaS Platform

One simple system that helps companies manage their people — employees, attendance, leave, and salary — while you run it as a subscription business. This guide explains the whole product in plain language, with zero technical knowledge required.

What is this platform?

It is an online Human Resource Management System (HRMS) rented by companies as a monthly or yearly subscription. Instead of every company buying and installing their own software, they all log in to one shared platform on the internet — and each company sees only its own private data.

Think of it like an apartment building. There is one building (the platform), but every company lives in its own locked apartment (its own private workspace). Neighbours can never see into each other's homes. You, the platform owner, are the landlord collecting rent.

🏢

For companies

A tidy place to manage staff, track attendance, approve leave, and run salaries.

How the SaaS subscription model works

A subscription means a company pays a recurring fee to keep using the platform — like Netflix, but for HR. The price is made of three simple parts.

The three parts of the price

  1. A base plan — a monthly (or yearly) fee that includes a set number of employee "seats" and a bundle of modules.
  2. Extra seats — if the company has more employees than the plan includes, each extra person costs a small per-seat fee.
  3. Add-ons — optional extras (like an additional module) can be added to a subscription.

What happens over the life of a subscription

1

Free trial

A new company tries the platform free for 14–30 days with full access.

2

Active

Once they pay, the subscription is active and renews each period.

3

Grace period

If a payment is missed, there's a short grace window where access continues while they sort it out.

4

Expired / paused

If still unpaid, access pauses. Their data is never deleted — it's simply locked.

5

Reactivated

The moment they pay again, everything comes back exactly as it was.

The golden rule
Pausing a subscription only switches off access. A company's employees, attendance history, and records are always kept safe and return instantly on reactivation.

Multi-tenant SaaS, in plain words

"Multi-tenant" simply means many companies share one platform but each keeps a private space. Going back to the apartment-building picture:

  • One building, many private apartments = one platform, many private company workspaces.
  • A company can never accidentally see another company's employees, salaries, or attendance.
  • The platform owner can see the building as a whole (how many companies, total revenue) — but staff inside a company only see their own company.

This design is what lets the business serve thousands of companies efficiently while keeping everyone's data separate and safe.

Module: Leave Management AVAILABLE

  • Define leave types (paid, sick, unpaid) and policies (how much accrues, carry-forward rules).
  • Employees request leave; managers and HR approve or reject it with a clear record.
  • Approved leave automatically reflects in attendance and in payroll deductions.
  • A shared calendar shows who is away and when.
Screenshot placeholderLeave request screen with balance remaining and an approval timeline.

Module: Payroll Management AVAILABLE

Turns attendance and salary structures into accurate monthly pay.

  • Set up each employee's salary made of components (Basic, HRA, allowances, deductions, tax).
  • Run payroll for a month: review → approve → process → publish payslips.
  • It automatically accounts for attendance, overtime, and unpaid leave.
  • Each employee gets a payslip they can download; HR gets summary and tax reports.
Designed to be fast
The goal is a payroll run completed in just a few clicks, with a guided review screen so nothing is missed.

How subscription billing works AVAILABLE

Billing is how the platform collects money from companies.

  • Each period, the platform creates an invoice for the company (base plan + extra seats + add-ons + tax/GST).
  • The company pays online through a payment gateway (such as Razorpay, Cashfree, or PayU).
  • Successful payments keep the subscription active; failed payments trigger reminders and a grace period.
  • Companies and the platform owner can view and download invoices.

User roles — who can do what

The platform recognises five kinds of users. Each sees a different version of the system suited to their job.

👑 Super Admin LIVE

The platform owner's team. Works across the whole platform (not inside any one company). Creates subscription plans and modules, manages all companies, handles billing and subscriptions, and views platform-wide revenue. Can step into a company to provide support.

🏢 Company Admin LIVE

The boss of one company's workspace. Controls company settings, the subscription and billing, user roles, and has full access to every module the company has subscribed to.

👩‍💼 HR Manager

Runs day-to-day HR: manages employees, attendance, and leave, and runs payroll. Cannot change billing or the subscription.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Manager

Looks after their team: approves their team's leave, views team attendance and profiles, and sees their own payslip.

🙋 Employee

Self-service only: marks their own attendance, requests leave, downloads their own payslips, and updates their profile.

Frequently asked questions

Is my company's data private from other companies?
Yes, completely. Every company has its own walled-off workspace; no company can ever see another's data. This separation is built into the core of the platform.
What happens to our data if we stop paying?
Access is paused, but your data is never deleted. The moment you reactivate, everything returns exactly as it was.
Can we start without paying?
Yes. Every plan comes with a free trial (14 days on Starter/Growth, 30 days on Enterprise) with full access.
Do we have to buy every module?
No. You only pay for the plan you choose, and you can add modules later as you grow.
What if we hire more people than our plan includes?
You can add extra "seats" for a small per-employee fee, or upgrade to a bigger plan.
Does payroll account for leave and absences?
Yes — when Attendance and Leave are active, payroll automatically reflects working days, overtime, and unpaid leave.
Can employees use it themselves?
Yes. Employees get a self-service portal to check in, request leave, and download payslips — reducing the load on HR.
Which payments are supported?
The platform is designed to work with popular gateways like Razorpay, Cashfree, and PayU. Online payment screens are part of the upcoming billing release.

A note on readiness (full transparency)

What works today vs. what's coming

This guide describes the full product vision. To be completely honest with stakeholders, here is exactly where things stand right now:

Built and working (the engine + the app): a public marketing website with live pricing and a contact form; a Super Admin owner console (platform analytics, company management, secure "log in as company", plan & pricing management, and a contact-inquiry inbox); a light/dark theme; and the full company app — sign-up and login, dashboards, and screens for Employees, Attendance, Leave, Payroll and Subscription/Billing, plus in-app notifications and downloadable CSV reports. Behind it: private workspaces, secure logins, the five user roles and permissions, the subscription plans and rules (seat limits, upgrade/downgrade, cancel/reactivate), billing with GST invoices and a real Razorpay integration, and automatic subscription lifecycle. The back end is covered by an automated test suite (46 tests) and the front end builds cleanly.

Still coming (deeper features): a full employee document vault and extended personal/salary fields, attendance break tracking + correction approvals + GPS/biometric capture, payroll period-locking with adjustment audit trails, additional payment providers (Cashfree/PayU), PDF/Excel report exports, email/SMS delivery, and two-factor login.

In short: the product is usable end-to-end today — sign up, add your team, track attendance, request and approve leave, run payroll, and manage your subscription, all from the browser.
